Highly Sensitive Sensors down to 100s of PPB

Our sensors can identify even the smallest amount of concentration, down to hundreds of parts per billion (PPB). For crucial applications including industrial quality control, medical diagnostics, and environmental monitoring, this degree of sensitivity is necessary.

Single & Multi Gas monitoring Sensors

Multi Gas monitoring Sensors: Can detect and monitor many types of gases simultaneously, whereas
Single Gas monitoring Sensors: Are specialized for detecting just one type of gas at higher sensitivity levels

Maintenance free

Maintenance-free sensors eliminate the need for regular calibration, cleaning, and adjustment. They provide reliable operation, reducing expenses and downtime.

Robustness and higher longevity

Our sensors life time is 5x better than the conventional ones and are robust to hazardous environments.
